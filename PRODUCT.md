# S Garage

## Register

product

## Users

ETS2 players editing locally installed trucks and trailers, including players with hard disks who wait for their first import.

## Product Purpose

Preview and edit owned vehicles using installed game, DLC, and selected mod parts. Support swaps, duplicates, paint, attachments, undo, and save backups.

## Brand Personality

A practical garage tool. Keep the existing interface style and use plain descriptions of the work underway.

## Anti-references

Loading states that leave players guessing whether the app has stopped. Invented percentages or completion estimates for work whose size is unknown.

## Design Principles

- Keep the truck editing workflow available and recognizable.
- Show the current loading stage and real completed counts where available.
- Explain first imports and cache reuse in ordinary language.
- Preserve save data and support recovery from errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Label loading status and progress bars for assistive technology. Keep text readable and respect reduced motion.
